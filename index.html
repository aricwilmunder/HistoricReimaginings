Historic Reimaginings explores how museums, historic sites, archives, and educators can responsibly use emerging technologies—including artificial intelligence, QR experiences, and digital storytelling—to deepen public engagement while maintaining historical integrity.

Using technology to help people engage more deeply with history—not replace it.
